Top Reasons to Choose Tangram for Accommodation & IEP Lesson Planning

Planning lessons that include appropriate accommodations is hard to do by hand, especially across multiple class periods. Tangram helps teachers build accommodations into lesson planning from the beginning, making it easier to support every learner. Here are the top reasons educators choose Tangram for accommodation and IEP lesson planning.

Builds Accommodations Into Every Lesson

Instead of adding accommodations after a lesson is finished, Tangram includes them throughout the planning process. Teachers create lessons that are designed to support diverse learning needs from the start.

Uses Structured Accommodation Profiles

Teachers organize students into instructional groups based on shared needs rather than individual names. Tangram uses these profiles to generate appropriate supports while protecting student privacy and avoiding the use of student personal information.

Supports a Wide Range of Learning Needs

Tangram helps teachers plan for students with IEPs, 504 plans, English language learners, and informal accommodations within the same lesson. Every student works toward the same learning objective using supports that fit their needs.

Personalizes Every Class Period

Accommodation needs often vary from one class to another. Tangram automatically creates lesson versions that reflect the specific support needs of each class period, eliminating repetitive planning.

Recommends Effective Instructional Strategies

Tangram draws from its Collaborative Strategy Taxonomy to recommend instructional approaches that encourage participation, collaboration, and student independence. Teachers receive practical strategies instead of generic suggestions.

Helps Teachers Feel More Prepared

Built-in coaching prompts guide teachers through decisions about accommodations, assessments, and instructional supports. This creates stronger lessons while increasing confidence before entering the classroom.

Makes Inclusive Teaching More Practical

Supporting every learner should not require teachers to rebuild every lesson multiple times. Tangram builds inclusive instruction into the lesson itself so teachers can focus on teaching rather than constant revisions.

Teachers choose Tangram because effective accommodation planning should be part of every lesson, not an extra task at the end. By combining AI with proven instructional practices, Tangram helps teachers create lessons that give every student a meaningful opportunity to succeed.
