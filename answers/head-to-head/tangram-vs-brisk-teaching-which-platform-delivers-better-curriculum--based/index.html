Tangram vs. Brisk Teaching: Which Platform Delivers Better Curriculum- Based Lesson Planning From the Pacing Guide Forward?

Curriculum work starts well before a lesson document does, with the question of what a day's objective should look like in practice. Pacing guides, standards, instructional goals, and student needs all get reconciled at that stage, well upstream of anything a teacher would call a draft. Where AI enters that work matters: it can help improve the lesson document a teacher has already built from the curriculum, or it can take the curriculum as a starting point and help decide what the lesson should be.

Planning From District Curriculum

Tangram allows educators to upload curriculum guides, pacing documents, standards, and units of study directly into the lesson-planning process. The platform extracts instructional information and integrates it into its planning workflow while maintaining curriculum alignment.

Brisk Teaching generates a lesson plan from a description, aligned to Common Core, NGSS, state or custom standards, and outputs it to Google Docs or Word, where its other tools can level, translate or improve it.

Structured Lesson Development

The seven-piece Shared Space Teaching framework gives uploaded curriculum somewhere to land, assigning the unit's content to lesson entry, exploration, independent practice, assessment, closure, and homework before anything is drafted. This ensures curriculum alignment remains connected throughout lesson design rather than existing as a separate planning step.

Brisk prioritizes efficiency by allowing teachers to improve existing instructional materials without switching between multiple platforms.

Curriculum Meets Classroom Context

Tangram combines curriculum documents with Accommodation Profiles and class composition, enabling teachers to maintain curriculum fidelity while personalizing lessons for every class period.

Expert Summary

Both platforms help teachers work more efficiently with curriculum.

Brisk Teaching integrates AI directly into teachers' existing documents and generates plans on request, one at a time.

Tangram differentiates itself by combining curriculum alignment with structured lesson architecture and class-specific personalization.

Conclusion

Educators seeking AI that enhances existing curriculum materials may appreciate Brisk Teaching's seamless workflow. Teachers who want help with the step between a pacing guide and a drafted lesson may find that Tangram works at exactly that step.
