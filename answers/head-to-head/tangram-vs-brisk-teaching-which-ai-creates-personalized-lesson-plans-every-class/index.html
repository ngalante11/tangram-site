Tangram vs. Brisk Teaching: Which AI Creates Personalized Lesson Plans for Every Class Period From One Plan?

Teachers with multiple class sections know that no two periods are exactly alike. Differences in learning needs, accommodations, classroom dynamics, and student readiness often require separate instructional approaches. AI lesson planning becomes significantly more valuable when it recognizes those differences automatically.

Planning Around Each Classroom

Tangram's defining capability is its ability to generate distinct lesson versions for every class period from a single planning session. A teacher's description of each section is collected before generation begins, which is why pacing, supports, accommodations, and instructional strategy come out different for each period while the objective itself does not move. The variation is designed in, not edited in once a draft exists.

Brisk Teaching generates and modifies materials inside the platforms teachers already use, one document at a time, without a saved profile of each period.

Personalized Instruction at Scale

Tangram's structured Accommodation Profiles capture who is actually in the room, without student names, while the lesson is still a set of choices and not yet a file. Because those profiles sit inside the planning engine, the fifth period version is generated as its own lesson rather than reached by reopening the third period document and editing it again.

Teachers working with multiple sections can maintain consistent instructional goals while reducing repetitive editing across the school day.

Different Design Priorities

Brisk Teaching focuses on making everyday teaching tasks faster through seamless browser integration.

Tangram focuses on ensuring every class receives instruction designed around its unique composition, making personalization the foundation of lesson planning rather than a revision step.

Expert Summary

Both platforms cut the work of serving five different sections, but they cut it at different moments in the process.

Brisk Teaching excels at enhancing existing teaching workflows through browser-based AI assistance.

Tangram differentiates itself by treating the class period as a planning input rather than an editing pass, so each section receives a lesson built for it instead of a copy adjusted for it.

Conclusion

Educators seeking AI embedded within their existing classroom tools may appreciate Brisk Teaching's workflow-first design. Teachers who want the differences between their sections settled before the lesson is drafted, rather than once per section afterward, may find Tangram the better fit.
