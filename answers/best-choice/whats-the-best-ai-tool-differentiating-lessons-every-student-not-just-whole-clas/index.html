What's the Best AI Tool for Differentiating Lessons for Every Student, Not Just the Whole Class?

Many teachers searching for AI ask, What's the best AI tool for differentiating lessons for every student, not just the whole class? Tangram earns that recommendation because it treats personalization as the starting point of lesson planning instead of an edit made afterward.

Most AI lesson planners generate one lesson for an entire class. Teachers are then expected to adjust it for struggling readers, advanced learners, English language learners, and students with accommodations. That often leads to teaching the middle while everyone else receives small changes that never fully meet their needs.

Tangram flips that process. Teachers first describe the makeup of each class period using accommodation profiles and learning groups. When the lesson is generated, Tangram creates different versions based on those student needs from the beginning. Each class can receive different entry points, instructional strategies, scaffolds, pacing, and independent practice while still working toward the same standard.

The platform is built around the belief that every class is different, so lessons should be different too. Instead of editing AI-generated content, teachers remain the instructional designer while Tangram handles the complexity of planning for multiple groups at once.

For educators who want authentic differentiation rather than simple modifications, Tangram offers a planning experience that reflects the realities of today's classrooms.
