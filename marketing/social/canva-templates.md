## How to make a new post (the locked workflow)

1. Open the master that fits the post type.
2. In Canva: **File → Make a copy** (never edit the master itself).
3. Swap ONLY the text — headline, eyebrow, list items / columns, and keep the CTA.
   Layout, colors, and fonts are already locked, so nothing drifts off-brand.
4. Download as PNG (1080×1350) and load into Buffer with the matching caption.

That's ~30 seconds per graphic, always consistent.

## Why we built them by hand

Canva's AI (`generate-design`) is great for a hero visual + one headline, but it
unreliably drops structured copy — numbered lists, two-column comparisons, size
hierarchy. These four were hand-built in the editor so the text is exact and the layout
is fixed. That's what "locked" means here.

## Brand rules baked in (keep them)

- Eyebrow line in **pacific blue (#5FD0FF)**, left-aligned.
- Headline in **League Spartan white**, left-aligned.
- CTA footer: **"3 free lessons · gettangram.com"** bottom-left, white.
- Never use: empower, transform, revolutionize, seamless, solution, game-changer.
- No invented stats or fake quotes (see `01-messaging-voice.md`).
