## 4. Voice & tone

**We sound like:** the sharp, warm instructional coach who's actually taught — who
respects your time, never talks down to you, and has receipts.

| We are | We are NOT |
|---|---|
| Warm, direct, teacher-to-teacher | Corporate, buzzwordy, "edtech-y" |
| Confident about the method | Arrogant or hype-y |
| Specific (ELL, IEP, Danielson 3c) | Vague ("empower," "transform," "solutions") |
| Respectful of teacher intelligence | Preachy or savior-complex |
| Time-aware ("get your Sundays back") | Guilt-trippy about "doing more for kids" |

**Rhythm:** short sentences. Real classroom nouns. One idea per line. Second person
("you," "your class"). Contractions always.

**Words we love:** your class, adapt, observation-ready, methodology, build once,
Period 5, Sunday night, ELLs, IEPs, the version, get your time back.

**Words we avoid:** empower, revolutionize, cutting-edge, solution, leverage, seamless,
game-changer, AI-powered (say what it *does*, not that it's AI), synergy.

## 8. What we CAN say / CANNOT say _(guardrail — read before shipping)_

**CAN (substantiated):**
- The founder-experience and district numbers above (from the site — verify they're current).
- "Not trained on the internet" / "original methodology" (core, true claim).
- Framework compatibility (Danielson/Marzano/UbD).
- Pricing and trial terms exactly as listed.

**CANNOT (until we have receipts):**
- ❌ Specific outcome stats we haven't measured ("saves teachers 5 hours/week," "raises test scores") — no invented numbers.
- ❌ Named teacher testimonials or quotes we don't actually have. No fabricated reviews.
- ❌ Claims that Tan replaces teacher judgment. It's a *partner*, always.
- ❌ Comparisons that name a competitor's brand disparagingly. Compare to "generic AI tools" generically.
- ❌ Any Founding Member count that isn't the real number.

**When in doubt:** describe the mechanism ("rule-based, so every class gets its own
version") rather than promising an outcome we can't yet prove.
