# Tangram — 90-Day Marketing Strategy Brief

_Owner: Nicole · Objective window: Aug–Oct 2026 (back-to-school peak)_

## 1. The one objective

**Drive free-trial starts** (the "3 free lessons, no credit card" flow) from K-12
teachers — then let the product's adaptation magic convert them to paid.

Everything is measured against this. Awareness, followers, and traffic only matter as
inputs to trial starts.

### Why the timing is a gift
We're entering marketing during the single highest-intent window in the teacher
calendar: **August back-to-school**. Teachers are actively dreading the planning grind,
budgets reset, and "new year, new system" energy is real. We ride that wave.

## 2. The hero customer

**"Overwhelmed gen-ed teacher."** One real person we write to:

> Grades 6–8, teaches 5 periods of the same subject to wildly different classes —
> one has 8 ELLs, one is honors, one has six IEPs. Plans on Sunday nights and after
> her own kids are in bed. Dreads observation season because the lesson that works
> for Period 1 falls apart in Period 5. Has tried the generic AI tools and hates
> re-editing the same bland output five times. Doesn't have time to learn a
> complicated system. Would pay real money to get her Sundays back.

**What she needs to hear:** *You build the lesson once. Tangram makes the five versions.
And it's built on a real method, not internet sludge.*

**Where she is:** Instagram + TikTok (teacher creators), Facebook teacher groups,
Pinterest (planning), and Google at 9pm searching "how to differentiate a lesson for
mixed ability" and "Danielson 3c examples."

## 3. Positioning (the wedge)

> Other AI tools give you *a* lesson. Tangram gives you a lesson for **your** class —
> built on a methodology we created, then adapted automatically for every group you teach.

Two things competitors can't copy quickly:
1. **The methodology moat** — original IP, 50+ years, hundreds of districts. Not
   "trained on the internet."
2. **The adaptation engine** — one build → many class-specific versions at $0 per copy.

## 4. Funnel & how each channel earns its place

```
   DISCOVER            →      BELIEVE           →     TRY            →   CONVERT
   (SEO + Social)             (Site + Email)          (Free trial)       (Paid)
   ─────────────             ─────────────           ───────────        ───────
   Blog ranks for            Landing page +          3 free lessons     In-product +
   teacher pain             proof of method          no credit card     email nurture
   Social hooks +            Welcome email                              → Founding Member
   teacher-group value       builds trust                                or Starter
```

- **SEO blog** — captures the 9pm Google searches. Compounding, owns the long tail. Primary discovery engine.
- **Social organic** — pattern-interrupt hooks + genuinely useful teacher content. Drives trial + feeds retargeting later.
- **Email/lifecycle** — the conversion workhorse. Turns "started a trial" into "paid," and "just curious" into "founding member."

## 5. Channel plan

### SEO blog (compounding discovery)
- **Cadence:** 2 posts/week to start (front-load for back-to-school).
- **Target clusters:**
  1. *Differentiation* — "differentiate lessons for mixed-ability classes," "one lesson multiple levels"
  2. *Observation/frameworks* — "Danielson domain 3 lesson examples," "UbD stage 2," "Marzano lesson plan"
  3. *ELL/IEP adaptation* — "how to modify lessons for ELL students," "IEP accommodations in lesson plans"
  4. *Time/burnout* — "lesson planning taking too long," "teacher planning system"
- Each post ends with a soft CTA to the free trial + a lead magnet where relevant.

### Social organic
- **Instagram + TikTok:** 4–5 posts/wk. Hooks built on the "same lesson, 5 classes, one Sunday night" pain. Short talking-head or screen-capture of Tan adapting a lesson.
- **Facebook teacher groups:** value-first participation (not spam) + 1 shareable resource/wk.
- **Pinterest:** pin every blog post + planning templates (evergreen search traffic).

### Email / lifecycle
- **Welcome sequence** (fires on trial start) — activate them to build lesson #1 fast.
- **Trial-conversion sequence** — the methodology story + a nudge before free lessons run out.
- **Founding Member drive** — scarcity ("first 500, price locked forever") to the not-yet-paid.

## 6. KPIs & targets (first 90 days)

| Metric | Why it matters | 90-day target* |
|---|---|---|
| Free-trial starts | The objective | Set baseline wk 1, then +20% MoM |
| Trial → paid conversion | Product proves itself | Track; optimize onboarding email |
| Blog organic sessions | Discovery engine health | Rank 3+ posts on page 1 of long-tail |
| Email trial-start rate | Nurture effectiveness | >2% of sends → trial |
| Founding Member slots filled | Urgency proof | Toward 500 |

_*Targets get real numbers once analytics (Amplitude/Supermetrics) are connected. Until
then we set a baseline in week 1 and grow from it — no vanity guesses._

## 7. What could go wrong (and the plan)

- **"Just another AI tool" skepticism** → lead with the methodology moat + "not trained on the internet," every time.
- **Teachers have no budget** → free trial removes risk; price is a coffee/month; annual plans for the committed.
- **Slow SEO ramp** → social + teacher groups carry discovery in weeks 1–8 while blog compounds.
- **Sending before we're ready** → nothing publishes/sends without connected accounts + your explicit approval.
