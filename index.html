Questions teachers ask

Tangram FAQ

What is Tangram?

Tangram is an AI lesson planning tool built by two career teachers. You build one lesson with Tan, Tangram's planning partner, and Tangram creates a differentiated version for each class you teach, with supports for IEPs, English learners and reading levels built in.

How does Tangram help me reach more of my students?

You build one lesson, then Tan builds a version for each class you teach, with supports for IEPs, ELLs, reading levels and MTSS tiers worked into every section. Same standard and outcome for everyone, a different way in for each group.

Does Tangram help with IEPs and English learners?

Yes. Tell Tan who is in each class, including students with IEPs, English learners and different reading levels, and every section of the lesson adjusts. Elevate also translates lessons and family communication into students' home languages.

Is the first month really free?

Yes. Every feature is included in your first month, not a stripped-down sample. No credit card to start, and nothing charges automatically when it ends. Pick a plan when you're ready, from $5.99/mo.

Do I have to upload student data?

No. Tangram doesn't need student rosters. Name a class "Period 2" or "Student A," tell Tan about the needs in the room, and your data stays yours.

Will Tangram work with my evaluation framework?

Yes. Lessons are already in the language of Danielson, Marzano and Understanding by Design, and Tangram covers state standards in every state.

How is Tangram different from other AI lesson plan generators?

Tan isn't trained on the internet. It runs on Shared Space Teaching, the methodology Tangram's founders built and refined across 650+ districts. On Elevate, you also get direct coaching calls with real educators.

How much does Tangram cost?

Your first month is free with every feature included. After that, Accelerate is $5.99/mo for 5 lessons a month and Elevate is $11.99/mo for unlimited lessons. Paying annually saves 44%. Schools and districts get custom Campus pricing.

Can my whole school or district use Tangram?

Yes. Campus gives your whole staff everything in Elevate plus admin tools, rostering, custom onboarding and building-wide trainings. Use the Inquire for Pricing form or book a school call.
